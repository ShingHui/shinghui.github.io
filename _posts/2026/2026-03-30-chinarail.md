High-speed rail in China is genuinely impressive — punctual, fast, and affordable. The official booking platform **12306** has an English version that works for international travellers. It takes a little patience the first time, but you'll get the hang of it quickly.

> Not ready to deal with the app? **Klook** and **Trip.com** are solid alternatives with full English interfaces. Slightly pricier, but totally hassle-free.
{: .prompt-tip }

---

## Before You Start

> You **do not** need a Chinese phone number to register. Your email is enough — Gmail works best.
{: .prompt-info }

> Only tickets for the **next 15 days** are available for booking, down to **30 minutes before departure**.
{: .prompt-warning }

## Step-by-Step: Booking on 12306

### 01 · Create Your Account

Go to **12306.cn** or download the **12306 app** (available on App Store and Google Play). Register with your email address.

> Check your **spam folder** — the verification email almost always ends up there.
{: .prompt-warning }

![12306 Register Screen](/assets/img/2025/china/12306/register.png){: w="400" }
_Registration page — choose email sign-up_

---

### 02 · Verify Your Identity

Choose **"Foreign Passport"** as your ID type, upload your **passport data page**, then take a selfie holding your passport. The app reads and auto-fills your details — just review them carefully before confirming.

> The ID verification is actually quite smooth. Camera translator helps a lot if you get stuck on any Chinese labels.
{: .prompt-tip }

---

### 03 · Add Your Passengers

Once verified, go to **My → My Passenger** and save your details. You can add up to **15 passengers** per account. All passengers must complete real-name verification before they can book.

![My Passenger Screen](/assets/img/2025/china/12306/passenger.jpg){: w="400" }
_Add passenger details here_

---

### 04 · Search for Your Train

Enter your origin, destination, and travel date, then tap **Search**.

> **Tricky part:** Multiple stations in one city can share the same pinyin name. To avoid confusion, find the **Chinese characters (汉字)** of your exact station and paste them into the search bar. Cross-check the train number and duration on **Trip.com** if unsure.
{: .prompt-danger }

> **Station tip:** Many cities have multiple stations — North / South / East / West. Always pick the one closest to your hotel or the city's main hub. Choosing a smaller station can easily add **30+ minutes** to your journey.
{: .prompt-warning }

![Train Search Screen](/assets/img/2025/china/12306/search.png){: w="400" }
_Train results show number, duration, departure/arrival time, and remaining seats_

---

### 05 · Select Seats & Confirm

Tap your preferred train → **Book** → select your passengers. Note that **seats are assigned randomly** by the system.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Max tickets per order | 5 single / 5 return |
| One ticket per passenger | Per train, per day |
| Seat assignment | Random — cannot choose |

![Train Search Screen](/assets/img/2025/china/12306/ticket.jpg){: w="400" }
_Train results show number, duration, departure/arrival time, and remaining seats (ticket purchase process through a mobile app)_ 


> Avoid the **standing ticket (无座)** unless you genuinely want to stand for hours. Trust me on this one.
{: .prompt-tip }

---

### 06 · Pay

Select **"International Card"** or **China UnionPay** as your payment method. Alipay/Wechatpay works too if you've set it up.

> All tickets are sold in **RMB (¥) only** on 12306. International card refunds may take up to **4 days** to process.
{: .prompt-info }

> Foreign cards (Mastercard, Wise debit) work on Alipay. However, payments above RMB 200 may incur a 3% transaction fee. For large bills, consider splitting payments or using cash.
{: .prompt-danger }

![Payment Screen](/assets/img/2025/china/12306/payment.jpg){: w="400" }
_Payment page — select International Card or Alipay_

After payment, a confirmation email will be sent to your registered email. You can also view your order under **"Order"** in the app.

## FAQs

**Can I buy tickets at the station on the day?**
Yes, but it's risky during peak season — trains sell out fast. If you do, have your train number, date, and station name written down. English is rarely spoken at ticket counters.

**When can I confirm my booking?**
12306 releases tickets 15 days before departure, so that's the earliest you can confirm. Occasionally, some trains release tickets later — 3 to 5 days before departure.

**How do I cancel a ticket?**
Go to **My → Order** in the app and submit a cancellation request. Refund timing depends on how far in advance you cancel — check the T&Cs before proceeding.
